💡 How to Download HLS (.m3u8) Streams

Modern streaming sites slice videos into hundreds of small chunks (.ts files) indexed by a playlist (.m3u8). Standard downloaders can't download them directly. Here is how to stitch them perfectly:

1
Copy Stream URL

In StreamGrab, locate the captured M3U8 Stream item and click Copy Link.

2
Stitch with FFmpeg

Open your Terminal or Command Prompt and run the copyable command displayed on the stream card, e.g.:

ffmpeg -i "STREAM_URL" -c copy "video.mp4"
3
Direct Online Converters

Alternatively, paste the copied M3U8 link into client-side stream fetchers or web converters to download the compiled MP4 directly in your browser.

🚀 Optimize for Creator Platforms

Once you download your video files, make sure they are tailored for maximum conversions and smooth playback on your upload platforms:

  • Recommended Formats: Always aim for MP4 with H.264 video codec and AAC audio codec for universal compatibility.
  • File Sizes: Compress larger videos to improve load speeds and lessen server buffering for subscribers.
  • Stripping Metadata: StreamGrab downloads direct video assets without external tracking identifiers, helping you protect your privacy automatically.
